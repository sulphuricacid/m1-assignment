What to Bring on Adoption Day

  • A valid photo ID showing your Georgia address
  • Proof that your landlord allows pets (if you rent)
  • A secure pet carrier for cats and small animals, or a leash and collar for dogs
  • All household members — everyone should meet the new family member first!

Preparing Your Home: First-Week Checklist

  1. Set up a quiet "safe space" where your new pet can decompress.
  2. Buy food, bowls, bedding, and a few toys before pick-up day.
  3. Pet-proof the house: secure cords, chemicals, and small swallowable objects.
  4. Book a first check-up with a local vet within the first two weeks.
  5. Be patient — the 3-3-3 rule says pets need 3 days to decompress, 3 weeks to settle, and 3 months to feel at home.
